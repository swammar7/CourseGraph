Help and keyboard shortcuts

Hover or focus any i for an explanation. Right-click, long-press or press the menu key on a section, a list item or a text box for its actions. The side panel follows the page you are on.

Alt + 1Show or hide the section tree
Alt + 3Show or hide the side panel
Alt + → / ←Next or previous page
Alt + EnterEnlarge or shrink the text box you are in
Shift + F10Actions for the focused item
← → in the tabs, ↑ ↓ in the tree and menusMove between items
EscClose a menu, a tip or the enlarged box
